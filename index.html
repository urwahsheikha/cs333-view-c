Q1 · Which community has the highest number of sales?
Al Barsha South Fourth recorded the highest number of unit and villa sales, with 3,925 transactions between January 2015 and August 2026.

Q2 · Is the map effective at identifying the highest-sales community?
Not directly. Community colors encode median sale price per m², not the number of sales. Counts are available through tooltips, but comparing communities one by one is difficult. The map is better suited to comparing property prices across Dubai; a sorted bar chart would be more effective for ranking sales volumes.

Data & method
